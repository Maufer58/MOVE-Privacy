## 2. Nature of the service

The application is published as **Stride** (formerly MOVE Fitness / M.O.V.E.).

Stride is a **fitness** app (running, gym and Apple Watch training) and **workout-planning** tool. It is **not a medical device** and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or medical advice.

User-entered “focus areas” / physical limitations are **self-declared training preferences**, not clinical records. If such data qualify as health data (GDPR Art. 9), processing is based on **explicit consent** (Art. 9(2)(a)).

## 3. Categories of data

Account/profile data; workout plans and history; optional body metrics; self-declared focus areas; technical preferences; subscription status (App Store / RevenueCat); HealthKit/Watch data if authorised; photos for optional paper-sheet import; AI prompts if AI features are used.

We do **not** collect payment card data ourselves (Apple handles payments).

## 4. Purposes & legal bases

Provide the fitness service (Art. 6(1)(b)); manage subscriptions; Apple Watch sync (consent/authorisation); optional AI features (consent/contract); security and diagnostics (Art. 6(1)(f)); legal compliance (Art. 6(1)(c)).

No sale of personal data to brokers; no third-party advertising profiling by the Controller.

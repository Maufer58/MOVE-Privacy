PRIVACY POLICY

Application: M.O.V.E. - Your Virtual Personal Trainer
Last updated: April 20, 2026

> This Privacy Policy describes how M.O.V.E. collects, uses, and protects User information. Our philosophy is based on an **"Offline-First"** approach: almost all data remains physically confined to the User's device.
### 1. DATA COLLECTION AND PROCESSING M.O.V.E. is designed to operate without requiring account creation or login. We do not proactively collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers for the App's core functionality. **The consent provided through the initial disclaimer authorizes the local data processing necessary for the App to function as described in this policy.** Data entered by the User (e.g., workout details, timers, history) is saved locally on the device via internal databases. The Developer does not have access to this data and does not store it on proprietary servers.
### 2. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND THIRD-PARTY DATA TRANSFER The App includes an "Import from Photo" (AI Wizard) feature. To provide this service, M.O.V.E. uses Google Generative AI APIs (Google LLC). * When using this feature, the selected image is temporarily and securely transmitted to Google's servers for processing and text recognition only. * No identifying data associated with the image is transmitted. * Users are strongly advised not to capture images containing personal data, faces, ID documents, or sensitive information.
### 3. HEALTH DATA ACCESS (APPLE HEALTH) The App, in its versions for wearable devices (e.g., Apple Watch), may request permission to read and/or write data to the Health app (Apple Health). * This data is used **exclusively** to provide real-time feedback during workouts. * **Non-sharing rule:** Data acquired through Apple Health is NOT stored on our servers and is NOT used for advertising or marketing purposes.
### 4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS To ensure proper functionality, the App requires access to: * **Camera/Photo Library:** Exclusively for the "Import from Photo" AI feature. * **Local Storage:** To save workout databases and export files.
### 5. ROLE OF PROFESSIONALS (TRAINERS) Trainers using the App to manage third-party clients act as the **Data Controllers** for the data entered. The Developer acts solely as a technology provider and has no access or responsibility for how the Trainer manages such data.
### 6. PROTECTION OF MINORS **M.O.V.E. is intended exclusively for an audience aged 18 and over (and in any case, of legal age in their jurisdiction of residence).** We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware of a minor using the App, we encourage parents to immediately uninstall the App.

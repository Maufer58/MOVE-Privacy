# PRIVACY POLICY

Application: M.O.V.E. Fitness
Last Updated: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the M.O.V.E. application ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. We designed M.O.V.E. with your privacy as our absolute priority through a decentralized architecture.

1. Local Data Collection & Storage (Offline-First)

M.O.V.E. operates on an "Offline-First" architecture. This means all your personal data, biometric data (weight, height, body composition), and workout history are saved exclusively locally on your device's memory.

  • We do not own, manage, or send this data to proprietary databases or cloud servers.
  • The User is the sole owner and is solely responsible for their data.
2. Artificial Intelligence & Third-Party Services

To offer advanced features (AI Wizard and Photo Import), M.O.V.E. uses Google Gemini APIs:

  • Ephemeral Processing: Text prompts or images are sent to Google servers exclusively for instantaneous processing.
  • No Retention: No images or identifying data are permanently saved in our systems or third-party systems after processing.
3. Health Data Integration (HealthKit / Health Connect)

M.O.V.E. requests access to frameworks like Apple HealthKit exclusively to read operational metrics (heart rate, active energy) in real-time during your workouts.

  • No Data Brokers: Health data will never be sold, shared, or licensed to third parties or profiling agencies.
  • No Advertising: The use of health data for marketing, retargeting, or advertising purposes is strictly prohibited.
  • Local Isolation: The acquisition of vital parameters occurs only during the session and remains confined to the device's encrypted database.
4. Device Permissions
  • Camera: Required to scan paper workout routines via AI.
  • Gallery: Required to allow the upload of a custom logo (Trainer version only).
  • Location (GPS): Exclusively used to track distance during outdoor cardio workouts.
5. Limitation of Liability

In accordance with Article 6 of the EULA, the User acknowledges that the execution of exercises occurs under their full responsibility. The App does not provide medical advice and should not be used unless the User is in adequate physical condition. In case of pain or discomfort, the User must immediately stop the activity.
